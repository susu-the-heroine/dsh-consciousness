# Conservative management of a dislodged leadless pacemaker wedged in the pulmonary artery assessed by pulmonary perfusion scintigraphy: a case report.

## Abstract

## Discussion

This case illustrates that conservative management may be feasible in carefully selected high-risk patients when an embolized leadless pacemaker is stably wedged within the pulmonary artery. Serial pulmonary perfusion scintigraphy may provide objective evidence of preserved pulmonary perfusion and support long-term follow-up after conservative management.
